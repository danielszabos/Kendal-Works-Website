# Kendal Works Website

Build a clean, professional marketing website for a serviced-office business. It should feel modern, trustworthy and corporate, with plenty of whitespace and a strong first impression. Mobile-first and fully responsive.

BUSINESS

Kendal Works (NW) Ltd rents serviced offices to local businesses in Kendal, Cumbria. The audience is local business owners looking for professional, flexible office space. The one goal of the site is to look credible and get visitors to phone. The main call to action everywhere is a phone call.

COLOURS (use these only, no other colours)

- Primary Blue #0C2B6B: headings, primary buttons, header, footer, key accents

- Primary Grey #5A5A5A: body text

- Secondary Grey #8E8E8E: secondary text, captions, labels

- Accent Grey #B8B8B8: borders, dividers, subtle lines

- White #FFFFFF: main background

Use white as the main background with occasional very light grey section bands for contrast. Keep it disciplined: blue and greys only.

STYLE AND TYPE

Clean, modern sans-serif typography. Generous spacing, clear hierarchy, rounded corners on cards and buttons, soft shadows used sparingly. Professional office-photography style for all imagery. High contrast for readability and accessibility.

NAVIGATION

Sticky header: logo on the left, nav links (Home, About, Gallery, Contact), and a prominent "Call 07944 883126" button on the right that is tap-to-call on mobile. Footer with the business name, phone number, a short line about the business, and quick links.

PAGES AND SECTIONS

Home

- Hero: short bold headline, one line of supporting text, a prominent call button, a strong office image.

- Why Kendal Works: three feature cards (flexible terms, central Kendal location, everything included), each with an icon or image, heading and short text.

- About the space: an image beside two short paragraphs, with a link to the About page.

- A look inside: a preview strip of gallery images linking to the Gallery page.

- Testimonial: a short quote from a happy tenant.

- Final call to action: a blue band with a heading and a call button.

About

- Intro heading and one or two lines.

- Our story: image beside two short paragraphs.

- What we're about: three value cards.

- A simple stats band (e.g. number of offices, access hours, one simple monthly price).

- Call to action.

Gallery

- Short intro.

- Photos grouped into sections: Offices, Meeting rooms, Common areas, each a responsive grid.

- Call to action.

Contact

- Phone-first: lead with the phone number and a call button.

- Contact details: phone, address, opening hours.

- A map of the Kendal location.

- Optional: a simple contact form (name, email, message). No account or login.

CONTENT

Write realistic placeholder copy suited to a serviced-office business, not lorem ipsum. Use professional placeholder office images that crop neatly to fixed sizes so the layout stays consistent. A logo will be supplied: leave clear space for it in the header and hero, and use a "Kendal Works" wordmark as a stand-in for now.

CONSTRAINTS

Keep it to these four pages. Do not add an online shop, checkout, booking system, pricing tables, blog or user accounts. This is a simple informational site whose job is to look professional and prompt a phone call.
